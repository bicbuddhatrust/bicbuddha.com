About Us

Rooted in Compassion, Built for Timeless Peace

Bodhi International Center (BIC) is a non-profit spiritual, educational, and humanitarian sanctuary dedicated to mindfulness meditation, classical Buddhist scholarship, and transparent global philanthropy. Our mission is to create an open refuge where seekers from every nation can practice stillness and service together.

Architectural Roadmap

4 Phases of Temple Construction

Sacred Land & Foundation
